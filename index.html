About the cameras. The Live cam links open third-party feeds run by SurfChex and the Outer Banks visitors bureau — free, no login. They are not embedded here because this page is sandboxed against loading outside video, and they go dark in storms, at night and out of season, so a cam that will not load is usually the camera rather than the link.
